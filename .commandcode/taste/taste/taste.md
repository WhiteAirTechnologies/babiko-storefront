# Taste
- Prefers minimal, restrained animations with a "premium" feel — subtle motion (gentle fade/rise, eased transitions) over flashy or showy effects. Confidence: 0.7
- Pushes back on motion that feels snappy or aggressive and asks for it to be softened even when already subtle — err on the side of gentler easing and smaller displacement/blur by default. Confidence: 0.5
- Cares about visual polish of layout: expects consistent alignment (shared edges between columns) and an even vertical spacing rhythm, and will flag sections where alignment or spacing "doesn't look good." Confidence: 0.4
- Treats mobile as the primary design target (most customers are on phones), so mobile layouts deserve real design effort — not just collapsing/stacking the desktop layout — with attention to vertical space and scroll length. Confidence: 0.75
- Does their own visual QA at mobile/small-viewport sizes and expects responsive layouts to render correctly (e.g. cards not clipped, content not overflowing), reporting issues via screenshots. Confidence: 0.4
- Expects equivalent section headings across the site to share identical typography (font size, line-height, letter-spacing) and will ask for them to be matched rather than left to drift apart via per-section overrides. Confidence: 0.4
- Wants on-page copy to match the site's existing brand voice and vocabulary rather than generic/invented filler (e.g. "according to our website"). Confidence: 0.4
